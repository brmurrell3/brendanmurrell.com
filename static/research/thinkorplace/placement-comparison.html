Curvature-cascade case studies (historical, v1 lineage)

Each view shows the hard macros (the large, fixed-size blocks) of one public IBM test design at their saved positions before and after a curvature-cascade refinement. These are saved placements from the May 2026 curvature-cascade experiments of the v1 predecessor, not the ranked 20 May pipeline. The straight segments join each macro's two saved positions and show displacement, not the path the solver took; soft clusters and standard cells are not drawn.
